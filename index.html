Qidi in Japan

Hi, I'm Qidi.

Everything happens for the best. At 39, I moved from Beijing to Osaka, Japan, and began a new chapter of life and entrepreneurship here. Since March 2023, I've been chronicling that transition — first on Xiaohongshu as "Qidi in Japan," and now on my independent blog.

I write about everyday life in a foreign land, the real experience of building businesses in Japan, and photography — capturing the moments of this era through my lens. Our generation stands at a turning point in history; from "A Beijinger in New York" to "A Beijinger in Osaka," the story never stops — it just changes stages.

Change. Create. Capture.

This is my personal sharing space — documents, reports and pages I share with friends and collaborators live here. If I sent you a link, you're in the right place.
